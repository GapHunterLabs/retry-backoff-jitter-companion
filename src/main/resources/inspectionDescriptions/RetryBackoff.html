Reports a retry with a fixed wait between attempts instead of exponential backoff with jitter: a manual for/while retry loop that calls Thread.sleep(constant) in its catch, Spring @Retryable without an exponential @Backoff, or a Resilience4j RetryConfig with a fixed .waitDuration(...) and no .intervalFunction(...).

When many instances retry on the same schedule, they hit the struggling service at the same moments and can keep it down (the "thundering herd" effect).
